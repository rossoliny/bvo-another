TEST COMMANDS
-------------

Gameplay cheats require personal activation with -cheats bvo-rossoliny, including single-player games. Only the activating player receives access. These commands are excluded from release maps.
-level <1..99> sets your own main hero's level, including lowering it. Example: -level 25.
-giveitem <rawcode> [number 1–10] adds an item directly to your own hero's inventory when no number is provided, or to the hero at the specified position in the scoreboard, including enemy bots. Examples: -giveitem bspd, -giveitem bspd 6. Invalid codes or numbers, missing heroes and full inventories do not create an item.
-gold <1..100000000> and -lumber <1..100000000> add gold or medals only to the player who entered the command. Examples: -gold 10000, -lumber 1000. Number format and overflow are checked.
-nomana without arguments toggles mana restoration for your own main hero every 0.1 seconds and after casting an ability. The setting persists through transformations. -mana is no longer used.
-nocd without arguments toggles cooldown resets for your own main hero after casting an ability. This replaces the former -nc command.
-oneshot without arguments toggles the mode for all your units: positive damage dealt to an enemy triggers bonus damage equal to 100% of the target's maximum HP.
For -nomana, -nocd and -oneshot, the first use enables the mode and the next use disables it.
-refresh without arguments resets ability and item cooldowns for your own main hero once per command.
-event without arguments starts preparation for a random available event without waiting for the timer. -event1, -event3 and -event5 request 1v1, 3v3 and 5v5. Fixed formats require at least 1, 3 or 5 players per team; bots count. Requests are rejected before the game starts, while hero selection is incomplete, when the format is disabled, when there are too few players, or during preparation or combat.
-oz <1..11> replaces the current Oz with the requested stage without rewards for the removed unit. Killing it continues the normal sequence; the final Oz closes the exits until his death. Example: -oz 11. The command is unavailable before hero selection ends and in No Waygates mode.
Every cheat use displays a map system notification to all players for 10 seconds: a colored [ЧИТЫ] prefix, player name, command, parameters and result. Activation is also announced, with the activation code hidden from the notification.
Separate diagnostic commands and their launch conditions are documented in cheats/README.md.
