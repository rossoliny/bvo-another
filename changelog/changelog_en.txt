## Abarai Renji

- Normal abilities: casting delay reduced from 0.4 to 0.1 seconds.
- Q — Howl Zabimaru: weapon reach increased from 720 to 800.
- W — Hado #31: Shakkaho: explodes after 0.4 seconds and deals physical damage. Changing orders cancels the preparation. Stun increased from 0.1 to 0.5 seconds.
- W damages Renji himself only when he is inside the explosion area.
- E — Higa Zekko: attacks and abilities become available 1 second after casting instead of 2 seconds after the flight ends. E no longer prevents casting R.
- E projectiles pursue the target for up to 20 seconds instead of 10 and gain 5% of their starting speed each second. Renji dying no longer stops them.
- R — Bankai: Hihio Zabimaru: the initial sweep now stuns enemies for 1 second and knocks them back.
- R segments scatter in front of Renji in 1 second instead of 1.8 and slow enemies by 50% within a radius of 200. The slows do not stack and are inactive during T.
- Pressing R again after the initial sweep — Bankai Dismissal — returns the segments for free and lets Renji attack and cast Q and E again. It works during scattering but is unavailable during T.
- On return, R expiration or Renji’s death, each remaining segment explodes for 7.5 × total Strength within a radius of 200. Damage from multiple explosions stacks. Segments used for T disappear without exploding.

## Kurosaki Ichigo

- T — Vastolorde: its logic was ported from 1.1c and improved. Items should no longer be lost when dying in Hollow Form, whether killed by Enel’s R or any other cause.
- T — Vastolorde: Hollow Form bonuses now scale with ability level; added magic resistance.

| Armor | +30 | +32 | +35 | +37 | +40 |
| Strength and Agility | +50 | +60 | +70 | +80 | +90 |
| HP | +500 | +750 | +1000 | +1250 | +1500 |
| HP regeneration/sec | +75 | +82 | +90 | +97 | +105 |
| MP regeneration/sec | +50 | +55 | +60 | +65 | +70 |
| Magic resistance | 0% | 2% | 5% | 7% | 10% |

- Entering and leaving Hollow Form no longer resets item cooldowns.
- After Hollow Form ends, Ichigo can no longer move during duels, 3v3 or 5v5 while the other participants are immobilized.

## Enel

- R — Mamaragan: lightning now interacts with shields, damage blocking and reflection.
- Darkness Sphere and God's Sphere activations no longer trigger repeated Mamaragan lightning.

## Nami

- Nami and her cloud no longer disappear when she uses W during Mihawk’s T.

## Items and shops

- Added Demonic Boots and Moon Armor to Recipe Shop 3, and Demonic Armor to Recipe Shop 5.
- Zaraki no longer loses gold when trying to buy Kuma's Unique Book and cannot receive it through item transfer.
- Buying medals, gold, attribute, health and level books, and the +100 bonus now works while affected by Hex.

## Hero selection and Oz arena

- Available heroes no longer disappear from selection after repicking, including when Nami or Kuma uses abilities.
- When the final Oz appears, the arena exits close until he dies.

## Scoreboard

- Added a new scoreboard showing kills K, deaths D, level, gold per minute GPM, hero icons, team totals, game mode and match time. Enemy GPM is hidden.
- The scoreboard counts only real hero deaths. Statistics stop changing once the winner is determined.

## Sounds

- Added R/T sounds for Ichigo, Mihawk and Zangetsu, plus Q/W sounds for Ichigo in Hollow Form.
- Kill streak sounds now play.
