HERO CHANGES
------------

_____
Renji
	[General]
		Changes relative to the original Unryze 1.1a:
		Spell cast point reduced from 0.4 to 0.1 seconds, 25% of the original value. Normal abilities begin taking effect sooner.

	[Q]
		Changes relative to the original Unryze 1.1a:
		Maximum weapon length increased from 720 to 800. The hit radius around each segment remains 100; at full extension the outer hit boundary reaches 900 instead of 820, providing a 100-range margin beyond the farthest selected point.
		The swing direction is fixed toward the selected point when cast. Visual segments and damage checks use the same coordinates. Damage, mana cost and cooldown are preserved.

	[E]
		Changes relative to the original Unryze 1.1a:
		E restrictions on attacks and abilities end 1 second after casting, regardless of a hit or flight cancellation. In the original, recovery occurred 2 seconds after E ended.
		Maximum projectile lifetime increased from 10 to 20 seconds.
		Instead of constant pursuit speed, projectiles gain 5% of their initial speed every second: +50% after 10 seconds, +100% after 20 seconds.
		Each E flight stores its own target and projectiles. An old flight does not affect subsequent E, Q or R casts; an unreachable target ends the flight at the time limit, while removed or hidden targets and dead or removed projectiles trigger cleanup.
		Unlike the original, target death alone no longer cancels E: projectiles can reach an existing corpse and play the normal impact effect.

		Renji's death no longer cancels E: projectiles keep pursuing the target until impact or the 20-second limit. Fully removing the hero still ends the flight.
		Unlike the original, E does not block R, including the first second after casting. E recovery does not cancel active R/T restrictions.

	[R]
		Changes relative to the original Unryze 1.1a:
		As soon as the initial rotation ends and Renji can move again, pressing R returns the segments for free, including during their flight, and ends R restrictions on attacks, Q and E. Bankai Dismissal has a 75-second cooldown, matching the main R, with no initial delay before it becomes available.
		Manual return does not stop movement or interrupt the hero's current actions.
		The main R retains its 350 mana cost and 75-second cooldown from the first cast. Without manual return, segments disappear 6 seconds after scattering, as in the original.
		Manual return is unavailable during T; segments are removed normally after T. Returning, dying or starting T cancels the old R timer, preventing it from affecting a new R after a cooldown reset.

		Initial sweep hits use the WarStomp effect and sound from Ikkaku's Final Madness, including lethal hits. The shared Stampede hit effect is silent for this sweep; its visuals are preserved.
		Enemies hit by the initial rotation are now stunned for 1 second and pushed clockwise along the sweep. Knockback uses Sanji Q settings: 12 units every 0.03 seconds, two 0.2-second waits, the same allowed map regions, pathing and tree-clearing behavior. Each enemy is hit once per rotation.
		After scattering, segments slow enemy movement by 50% within 200 AoE. Overlapping segment auras do not stack, and the aura is disabled during T.
		On manual return or automatic expiration, every segment explodes within 200 AoE for 7.5 x current total STR damage. Overlapping explosions stack. Segments removed after T or on Renji's death do not explode. Uses a red ChaosExplosion effect per segment and the explosion sound from Renji's Higa Zekko once for the whole return.

	[Descriptions]
		Q: original 1.1a text with range 800; cast timing is omitted from the tooltip.
		E: shortened original text with correct Strength scaling, 250 mana and recovery after 1 second; flight details are omitted.
		R: original 1.1a text with a 1-second stun, knockback and 50% slow near the segments. The return button is named Bankai Dismissal and lists damage per segment, radius and a 75-second cooldown.
		References to other abilities use their names: Howl Zabimaru, Higa Zekko and Hikotsu Taiho.
		T: mechanics and descriptions match the original 1.1a; the signature is removed.

-------------
TEST COMMANDS
-------------

All cheats require personal activation, including single-player games. Only the activating player receives access.
The -level N and -createitem XXXX commands use only the command author's own hero.
Added -gold N and -lumber N to add the specified amount of gold or medals only to the player who entered the command.
Added -mana and -nomana to enable or disable infinite mana only for the player's own hero; the setting persists through transformations.
The -nc command resets cooldowns only for the enabling player's own hero.
Every cheat use displays a map system notification to all players for 10 seconds: a colored [ЧИТЫ] prefix, player name, command, parameters and result. Activation is also announced, with the activation code hidden from the notification.
